P01258 gene-related peptide is a potent inhibitor of DB05875 degradation . P01258 gene-related peptide ( P80511 ) was found to potently inhibit a DB05875 endopeptidase isolated from human P04141 . P80511 potentiated DB05875 irritant actions ; a possible mechanism is interaction for a common metabolic step . Somatostatin is another peptide capable of competing with DB05875 endopeptidase .
