Habituation in prepulse inhibition is affected by a polymorphism on the DB01221 receptor 2B subunit gene ( Q13224 ) . OBJECTIVES : To identify the reliable connectivity between causal genes or variants with an abnormality expressed in a certain endophenotype has been viewed as a crucial step in unraveling the etiology of schizophrenia because of the considerable heterogeneity in this disorder . METHODS : According to this practical and scientific demand , we aimed to investigate the relationship between seven top-ranked variants in the SZgene database [ 120-bpTR in P21917 , rs1801028 and rs6277 in P14416 , rs1019385 ( T200G ) in Q13224 , rs1800532 in P17752 , rs1801133 ( C677T ) in P42898 , rs2619528 ( P1765 ) in Q96EV8 ] and prepulse inhibition ( PPI ) and habituation after acoustic stimulus ( HAB ) . RESULTS : Both PPI and HAB were decreased significantly in patients with schizophrenia . In addition , we observed a significant effect of Q13224 ( human DB01221 receptor 2B subunit gene , Q13224 ) genotype on HAB ( P < 0.05 , not corrected ) . CONCLUSION : Although these findings need to be replicated in other samples , an underlying mechanism of impaired biological reaction may be influenced by DB01221 hypofunctioning in schizophrenia .
