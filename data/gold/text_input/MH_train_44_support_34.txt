[ Spiral computed tomography with functional tests in diagnosis of chronic obstructive diseases of the lungs ] . The potential of spiral computed tomography ( P09683 ) and high resolution computed tomography ( HRST ) in diagnosis of chronic obstructive pulmonary diseases ( P48444 ) is described . Semiotic sings of bronchial asthma ( BA ) and chronic obstructive bronchitis ( P00156 ) are specified . Informative value of P09683 in differential diagnosis of BA and P00156 including early stage of the diseases is analysed . An updated technique of P09683 and HRCT of the lungs in P48444 patients is presented .
