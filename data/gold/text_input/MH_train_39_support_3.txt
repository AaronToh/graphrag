Early DB09150 /PET scanning as a pharmacodynamic marker of anti- P00533 antibody activity in colorectal cancer . DB01269 is an anti- P01133 receptor ( P00533 ) antibody approved for use in treatment of chemotherapy-refractory colorectal cancers lacking K- DB01367 mutations . Despite overall response rates approximating 10 % , no marker predictive of clinical benefit has been identified . We describe a chemotherapy-refractory patient whose clinical condition necessitated rapid identification of an effective agent in whom we used (18)F- DB09150 -positron emission tomography ( DB09150 -PET ) /computed tomographic scanning 48 hours after an initial dose of panitumumab to document a pharmacodynamic response to the antibody . The initial 46 % ± 2.7 % drop in SUV(max) of four target lesions correlated with a partial response by Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors and a > 90 % drop in serum carcinoembryonic antigen at 8 weeks , indicating that an early decrease in DB09150 uptake may predict subsequent clinical benefit in response to anti- P00533 antibody therapy in colorectal cancer .
