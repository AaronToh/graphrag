O75144 costimulation is required for T-cell encephalitogenicity . The interaction of Q9Y6W8 with its ligand on P25054 provides a costimulatory signal to previously activated T-cells . In these studies , we blocked the Q9Y6W8 : O75144 interaction with Q9Y6W8 -Ig during the in vitro activation of MBP-reactive transgenic P01730 (+) T-cells . The presence of Q9Y6W8 -Ig in these cultures inhibited the ability of the transgenic T-cells to transfer EAE , although they entered the brains of the recipient mice . Q9Y6W8 -Ig increased apoptosis in the transgenic T-cells , especially in the memory population . This enhanced apoptosis was accompanied by an increase in the Q07812 /BCL-2 mRNA ratio . Q9Y6W8 -Ig did not prevent P60568 production , demonstrating that P60568 production is O75144 independent . P01579 and P22301 production by the transgenic T-cells , however , was suppressed . Finally , Q9Y6W8 -Ig injection into mice after the first signs of EAE ameliorated clinical disease . Therefore , O75144 provides a signal distinct from P10747 costimulation that is required for the activation and viability of encephalitogenic T-cells .
