DB01269 a novel drug in cancer treatment . The epidermal growth factor receptor ( P00533 ) is a member of the erbB family overexpressed in most of the solid tumors . In cancer cells , the overexpression of P00533 correlates with the development and the progression of tumor . DB01269 is a fully human monoclonal antibody that blocks the extracellular domain of the P00533 and has not been associated with the formation of any antibodies directed against it . This review summarizes on the preclinical and clinical development of panitumumab in human solid tumors . As bevacizumab and cetuximab have been approved for colorectal cancer because of their improvements in progression-free survival and overall survival when associated with chemotherapy , panitumumab represents an interesting molecule which needs more phase III studies to validate its efficacy .
