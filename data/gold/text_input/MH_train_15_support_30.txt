Feature-map vectors : a new class of informative descriptors for computational drug discovery . In order to develop robust machine-learning or statistical models for predicting biological activity , descriptors that capture the essence of the protein-ligand interaction are required . In the absence of structural information from X-ray or NMR experiments , deriving informative descriptors can be difficult . We have developed feature-map vectors ( FMVs ) , a new class of descriptors based on chemical features , to address this challenge . FMVs , which are derived from the conformational models of a few actives , are low dimensional , problem specific , and highly interpretable . By using shape-based alignments and scoring with chemical features , FMVs can combine information about a molecule 's shape and the pharmacophores it can match . In five validation studies , bag classifiers built using FMVs have shown high enrichments for identifying actives for five diverse targets : P24941 , 5-HT(3) , P00374 , thrombin , and P12821 . The interpretability of these descriptors has been demonstrated for P24941 and 5-HT(3) , where the method automatically discovers the standard literature pharmacophore .
