Concentrations of pravastatin and lovastatin in cerebrospinal fluid in healthy subjects . The capability of pravastatin and lovastatin , P04035 inhibitors likely to be taken chronically for hypercholesterolemia , to cross the blood-brain barrier was investigated in normal male volunteers . DB00227 , which is lipophilic , was detected in cerebrospinal fluid ( P04141 ) at concentrations that may have a pharmacologic effect . DB00175 , which is hydrophilic , was not detected in P04141 . It is concluded that pravastatin may have less potential for causing CNS-related side effects than lovastatin .
