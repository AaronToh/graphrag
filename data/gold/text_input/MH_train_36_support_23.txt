[ Chronic myeloid leukemia ] . More than 10 years have passed since imatinib as a first developed P11274 - P00519 tyrosine kinase inhibitor ( TKI ) introduced in treatment of patients with chronic myeloid leukemia ( CML ) . In globally , there are tremendous numbers of patients on imatinib therapy . Based upon randomized trials comparing second generation TKIs such as dasatinib and nilotinib versus imatinib , both TKIs produce faster and deeper response than imatinib and they can be selected as first-line therapy for newly diagnosed chronic phase of CML ( CP-CML ) as imatinib . Bosutinib is a potent for imatinib resistant/intolerant CP-CML and can be used as second or third-line therapy . DB08901 is the only clinically available TKI that has activity against the T315 mutation that is resistant to all other TKIs . Currently , a choice among these potent TKIs should take into consideration the drug side effect profiles and the patient 's comorbidities .
